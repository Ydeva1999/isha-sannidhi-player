## 📱 Quick Install on iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open this website in **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad:
   👉 **[https://ydeva1999.github.io/isha-sannidhi-player/](https://ydeva1999.github.io/isha-sannidhi-player/)**
2. Tap the Safari **Share button** at the bottom (the square with an arrow pointing up `⎋`).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen" (`होम स्क्रीन में जोड़ें`)** `[+]`.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.

> ✨ **That's it!** The authentic Sannidhi Player icon will now appear directly on your iPhone home screen. It runs in full screen without browser toolbars, works offline, and locks into the iOS Lock Screen and Dynamic Island!

## ✨ Features

- **Uncompressed Sacred Audio Tracks:** Full ~40-minute Hindi Sannidhi Pooja and English Sannidhi Pooja recordings.
- **Cupertino Frosted-Glass Settings (`⚙️`):**
  - **Hide Notification Popups:** Keeps screen calm and suppresses floating banners during Pooja.
  - **5 min Pre-Pooja Silence:** Preparation buffer (e.g., start at 06:10 for prompt 06:15 Pooja) with MM:SS countdown and instant start bypass.
  - **1-Hour Screen Sleep:** Automatically releases screen keep-awake after 60 minutes (~07:15) to prevent battery drain while audio continues.
- **Exact 06:15 Sandhya Kala Auto-Play:** Live clock synchronization for Morning 06:15 AM and Evening 06:15 PM with gentle authentic wakeup bell chime.
- **Dynamic Island & Lock Screen:** Full `MediaSession` integration with album art and playback controls.
- **Authentic Sannidhi Bell Chime:** Extracted directly from official Sannidhi recordings.
